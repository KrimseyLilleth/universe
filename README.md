# Krimsey Lilleth: Interactive Models

Interactive models that run in your browser, with nothing to install.

## Amphidromic Points Explained Visually

Places in the ocean where the tide barely rises or falls, even though high and low tide circle around them like the hands of a clock. Built on real NASA satellite tide data.

![Where the Tide Stands Still](liquid-universe/youtube_thumbnail_barrel.png)

- **Globe:** [open the model](https://krimseylilleth.github.io/universe/liquid-universe/amphidromic_earth.html)
- **Kiddie pool / close-up:** [open the model](https://krimseylilleth.github.io/universe/liquid-universe/amphidromic_closeup.html)
- **More info, controls and data sources:** [liquid-universe/README.md](liquid-universe/README.md)

## The Klimax Torus Ladder

An endless zoom through nested Klimax tori. The Klimax torus is the one ring shape (R/r = φ, the golden ratio) whose four natural radii are all powers of φ, so the same pattern repeats at every scale.

- **Animation:** [open the model](https://krimseylilleth.github.io/universe/liquid-universe/klimax_ladder_animation.html)
- **Paper:** *The Klimax Torus: The unique aspect ratio whose radii are powers of φ at every scale* ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.20821925](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20821925))
